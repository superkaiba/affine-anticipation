### Conversation corpora

The context prompts the pipeline streams come from two Hugging Face conversation corpora, named in `pipeline/README.md` and in `pipeline/scripts/capture_answers.py`. Neither corpus is redistributed here, in whole or in part as a bank: the capture steps download them at run time.

- `lmsys/lmsys-chat-1m` (https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmsys/lmsys-chat-1m). Released under a custom LMSYS-Chat-1M Dataset License Agreement rather than a standard open-source license. As stated on that dataset page, the agreement grants a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to use the dataset for research and commercial purposes, and it prohibits distributing, copying, disclosing, sublicensing, hosting or otherwise transferring the dataset to a third party. Access on the Hub is gated and the page warns that the conversations are unscreened and may be offensive.
- `allenai/WildChat-1M` (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/WildChat-1M). Released under ODC-BY, the Open Data Commons Attribution License, which allows sharing, modification and use subject to attribution. AI2 additionally asks that use follow its responsible-use guidelines, and notes that the dataset carries output generated by third-party models that is subject to those models' own terms.

One committed record quotes from those corpora. `records/issue_1901/content_divergent_retrieval_examples_10k.json` illustrates two retrieval failures, each paired with the candidate retrieved in its place, so it carries four short real-user queries, each present both as a display excerpt and as the full query text the excerpt was drawn from. The longest is 272 characters. The answers shown beside them are not corpus content: they are this work's own Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct generations for those queries, recorded with their SHA-256 digests and their generation seed in the same file.
